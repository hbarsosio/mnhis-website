Opportunities

Join Us

We welcome talented researchers, students, and professionals who share our passion for improving maternal and newborn health in Africa.

Whether you're seeking a PhD position, research fellowship, or collaboration opportunity, we'd love to hear from you. Our team works on cutting-edge implementation science across malaria, immunization, and health systems.

🎓

PhD Students

We supervise PhD students in collaboration with LSTM and other partner universities. If you have your own funding or wish to apply for a studentship, we welcome applications from candidates interested in maternal immunization, malaria in pregnancy, or implementation science.

🔬

Research Fellows

Postdoctoral researchers and fellows with interests in clinical trials, epidemiology, or health systems research are encouraged to apply. We can support fellowship applications to funding bodies including Wellcome, NIH, and EDCTP.

🤝

Research Collaborators

We partner with academic institutions, NGOs, and government agencies on research projects. If your organization is interested in collaboration on maternal, newborn, or adolescent health research in Kenya, please reach out.

👨‍💼

Visiting Researchers

We host visiting researchers and clinicians who wish to gain experience in implementation science research in Africa. Short-term and long-term visits can be arranged based on mutual interests and ongoing projects.
