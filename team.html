Dr. Hellen Barsosio

Principal Investigator & Group Lead

IMPROVE, IMPROVE-2, MIRNA, AGREE, MiMBa, SAFIRE

Kenyan medical doctor and clinical researcher specializing in maternal and newborn health. Dr. Barsosio leads nine studies across five counties in western Kenya, overseeing 60+ study sites and 35,000+ women. Her research focuses on ensuring babies are born healthy and strong through three pillars: maternal immunization, malaria prevention, and health systems strengthening.

🏆 Calestous Juma Fellow 🌍 Goalkeepers 2025 🎖️ ASTMH Young Investigator 2022 👩‍🔬 WomenLift Health
