Malaria in Pregnancy

Prevention and Treatment Interventions

LSTM - Dept. of Clinical Sciences Medicines for Malaria Venture

IMPROVE ↗

Intermittent Preventive Treatment Optimization - A multi-site trial evaluating enhanced malaria prevention strategies during pregnancy to improve maternal and birth outcomes.

ClinicalTrials.gov ↗

IMPROVE-2 ↗

Phase 2 evaluation of next-generation prevention strategies building on IMPROVE findings, testing novel drug combinations for enhanced efficacy.

ClinicalTrials.gov ↗

AGREE

L-arginine supplementation trial investigating vascular health improvements during pregnancy to optimize fetal growth and reduce adverse outcomes.

PI: Prof. Kevin Kain, University of Toronto
Kevin Kain Lab ↗ ClinicalTrials.gov ↗

MiMBa ↗

Malaria in Mothers and Babies - A pregnancy registry studying antimalarial safety and birth outcomes to inform treatment guidelines.

+ Medicines for Malaria Venture
Project Website ↗

SAFIRE ↗

Safety of Antimalarials in First Trimester - The first Phase III trial testing antimalarial safety in early pregnancy across multiple African countries.

+ Medicines for Malaria Venture
SAFIRE Consortium ↗

Adolescent Well-being

Mental Health & Support Programs

Stanford University Prof. Clea Sarnquist Lab

ALHIV ↗

Adolescents Living with HIV Support Program - Mental health and adherence support interventions for young people living with HIV.

Sarnquist Lab ↗

Friendship Bench ↗

Community-based mental health intervention adapted for Kenyan adolescents, providing accessible psychological support through trained lay health workers.
